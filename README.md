## 🇭🇰 中文說明 (Traditional Chinese)

### 📌 專案簡介
本專案為針對歐盟《人工智能法案》（Regulation (EU) 2024/1689）建置之專業雙語（繁體中文 / 英文）合規問答庫與法規導航系統。專為跨國企業法務、資料保護官（DPO）、AI 產品經理以及 IAPP AIGP（人工智慧治理專業人員認證）考生提供精準、結構化且具實務操作性的法規解析。

### 🧭 核心內容模組
1. **01 適用邊界 (Scope & Jurisdiction)**：解析域外效力（Extraterritorial Reach）與實質影響原則。
2. **02 角色界定 (Roles & Value Chain)**：界定 Provider、Deployer、Importer 與 Distributor 之法定責任邊界及角色升級陷阱。
3. **03 風險分級 (Risk Classification & GPAI)**：詳解 8 大不可接受風險禁用實踐、Annex III 高風險用例及通用 AI 模型（GPAI）治理規範。
4. **04 部署者義務 (Deployer Obligations)**：聚焦企業採購第三方 AI 時必須落地的 6 大法定責任（人工監督、日誌保存、員工告知等）。
5. **05 處罰與時間表 (Penalties & Timeline)**：三層罰款機制（最高 3,500 萬歐元 / 7% 全球營業額）與 2025–2027 階段性生效路徑圖。
6. **06 法律與治理聲明 (Legal & Governance)**：涵蓋 GDPR、香港 PDPO、ISO/IEC 42001 及 ISO/IEC 27001 之跨法域治理對齊聲明。

## 🇬🇧 English Overview

### 📌 Project Summary
This repository hosts a high-standard, bilingual (English & Traditional Chinese) compliance navigation platform for the **European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)**. It provides structured legal synthesis, decision frameworks, and operational guides for legal counsels, compliance officers, AI product teams, and candidates preparing for the **IAPP Certified Artificial Intelligence Governance Professional (AIGP)** credential.

### 🧭 Core Documentation Architecture
* **01 Scope & Jurisdiction**: Extraterritorial applicability, outputs doctrine, and research exemptions.
* **02 Roles & Value Chain**: Provider vs. Deployer responsibilities, third-party distributor duties, and upstream/downstream role conversion.
* **03 Risk Classification & GPAI**: 8 Prohibited practices, Annex III High-Risk AI systems, and systemic risk GPAI requirements.
* **04 Deployer Obligations**: Downstream compliance requirements, human oversight design, 6-month log retention, and worker notifications.
* **05 Penalties & Implementation**: Multi-tier penalty structure (€35M / 7%), SME protections, and enforcement timeline (2025–2027).
* **06 Legal & AI Governance**: Multi-jurisdictional alignment across EU GDPR, HK PDPO, ISO/IEC 42001:2023, and ISO/IEC 27001:2022.
