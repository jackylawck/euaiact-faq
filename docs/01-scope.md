# 01 適用邊界與管轄範圍 (Scope & Territorial Scope)

> **法定核心依據 / Legal Basis**: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 **Article 2 (Scope)**, **Article 3(1) (AI Definition)** & **Recitals 12, 21–24**

## 🇭🇰 適用邊界深度解析 (中文版)

### 1.1 地理與域外效力 (Geographic & Extraterritorial Reach)
* **核心條文**：Article 2(1)(c)
* **實質影響原則**：企業即使在歐盟**無分公司、無伺服器、無僱員**，只要系統產生的**輸出結果（Output）在歐盟境內被使用**，即完全受管轄。

### 1.2 實質技術定義邊界 (Material Scope: 什麼算 AI？)
* **核心條文**：Article 3(1)
* **AI 系統法定定義**：具備自主性（Autonomy）、適應性，能根據輸入推導出如何生成輸出（內容、預測、推薦或決策）的機器系統。
* **排除對象**：純基於明確規則的傳統演算法／腳本（Classic Rule-based Software）不受本法約束。

### 1.3 適用主體架構 (Subject Scope)
1. **Provider（提供者）**：投放市場或交付使用者（不論位於歐盟境內或第三國）。
2. **Deployer（部署者）**：在歐盟境內使用 AI 系統的主體。
3. **境外主體（Third-Country Entities）**：系統 Output 進入歐盟之境外開發者與部署者。
4. **進口商與分銷商（Importers & Distributors）**：供應鏈中間人。

### 1.4 法定全面豁免清單 (Statutory Exemptions)
* **純國防與軍事用途** (Article 2(3))
* **未上市前之純科研與原型開發** (R&D Phase - Article 2(6))
* **個人純日常生活/非職業使用** (Personal Activity - Article 2(10))
* **非商業且非高風險之開源 AI 組件** (Open-Source - Article 2(12))

### 1.5 跨境商業案例速查庫 (Case Studies)
* **案例 1（跨境招聘）**：亞洲總部 AI 篩選歐盟應徵者 $\rightarrow$ **受管轄且屬高風險**。
* **案例 2（Fintech 評級 API）**：亞洲輸出信用分予歐洲銀行 $\rightarrow$ **受管轄**。
* **案例 3（跨境客服）**：全球官網面向歐盟居民之 Chatbot $\rightarrow$ **受管轄（透明度義務）**。
* **案例 4（純本地運作）**：本地餐廳排隊預測系統 $\rightarrow$ **完全豁免**。

## 🇬🇧 Scope & Extraterritorial Reach (English Version)

### 1.1 Geographic & Extraterritorial Reach (Article 2(1)(c))
* **Effects Doctrine**: Non-EU entities are fully bound if the **output produced by the AI system is used in the Union**, regardless of physical establishment or server location.

### 1.2 Material Scope (What qualifies as an AI System? - Article 3(1))
* **Statutory Definition**: Machine-based systems designed to operate with varying levels of autonomy, inferring from inputs how to generate outputs (content, predictions, recommendations, or decisions).
* **Carve-out**: Traditional deterministic software and basic rule-based scripts are excluded.

### 1.3 Subject Scope (Value Chain Entities)
1. **Providers**: Placing on the market or putting into service (EU or third-country).
2. **Deployers**: Using AI systems within the Union in professional contexts.
3. **Third-Country Operators**: Where system outputs produce legal/practical effects in the EU.
4. **Importers & Distributors**: Supply chain intermediaries.

### 1.4 Statutory Exemptions (Article 2(3)–(12))
* **National Security & Defense**: Exclusively military AI (Art. 2(3)).
* **Scientific Research & Development**: Pre-market testing and prototyping (Art. 2(6)).
* **Purely Personal Activities**: Non-professional everyday usage (Art. 2(10)).
* **Free & Open-Source AI**: Conditional exemptions for non-high-risk OSS (Art. 2(12)).

### 1.5 Practical Case Studies
* **Case 1 (Offshore HR)**: Asian HQ filtering EU job applicants $\rightarrow$ **In-Scope (High-Risk)**.
* **Case 2 (Fintech Risk API)**: Exporting default probabilities to EU banks $\rightarrow$ **In-Scope**.
* **Case 3 (E-Commerce Chatbot)**: Generative bots interacting with EU consumers $\rightarrow$ **In-Scope (Transparency)**.
* **Case 4 (Domestic Operations)**: Local queue management with no EU nexus $\rightarrow$ **Out-of-Scope**.
