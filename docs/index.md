<span id="chinese-summary"></span>
## 🇭🇰 中文核心導覽

歐盟《人工智能法案》（EU AI Act）是全球首部全面約束 AI 系統的具體法規。其影響力媲美甚至超越 GDPR：

* **穿透性域外效力**：非歐盟企業只要將輸出（Output）用於歐盟境內，一律受管轄。
* **嚴苛罰則機制**：最高處 **3,500 萬歐元** 或全球營業額 **7%** 行政罰款。
* **採購方同擔責任**：採購現成商業 AI（如 HR 篩選、客服 Bot）的 Deployer 必須承擔法定運行與監督義務。

<span id="english-summary"></span>
## 🇬🇧 English Executive Summary

The European Union Artificial Intelligence Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689) represents the world’s first comprehensive binding horizontal legal framework for AI:

* **Extraterritorial Jurisdiction**: Non-EU entities are bound if their AI outputs produce practical or legal effects within the Union territory.
* **Severe Global Penalties**: Fines reach up to **€35 Million** or **7%** of worldwide annual turnover.
* **Deployer Accountability**: Purchasing off-the-shelf AI triggers mandatory duties regarding human oversight, 6-month log retention, and worker notifications.
